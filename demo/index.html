The rules

Every slide counts as one move. Robots cannot stop halfway or pass through one another. Passing over the target does not count.

Use 1–4 to choose a robot, then the arrow keys. Z undoes a move.

Based on the standard board and color rules in the Java original. Four practice puzzles; multiplayer bidding and the complex board remain in the desktop project.
